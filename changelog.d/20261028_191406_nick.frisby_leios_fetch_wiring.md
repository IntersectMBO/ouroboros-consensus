### Breaking

- The node fetches endorser blocks. Its `NodeKernel` runs LeiosFetch's
  decision loop and pruner, from
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Kernel`, and gains
  `getLeiosFetchKernel`. `NodeKernelArgs` gains `leiosFetchRng`.
- `Handlers`' `hLeiosFetchClient` and `hLeiosNotifyClient` take the peer's
  `LeiosFetchPeer`, in which the LeiosNotify client records the peer's offers.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.Tracers.Tracers` gains `leiosFetchClientTracer`.
- The node's rethrow policy treats an `ExnLeiosFetchInvalidReply` as the peer
  being buggy or evil.
- `onValidAnnouncement` says whether the announcement was new
  (`WhetherNewAnnouncement`).
