### Breaking

- `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes the `TxSubmissionLogicVersion`; `NodeKernelArgs`
  gains `txTierRng`, and `NodeKernel` gains `getTxSubmissionConfig`,
  `getFreshBytes` and `getFreshMaxBytes`.

### Non-Breaking

- Follow ouroboros-network's tx-submission configuration: the decision policy
  comes from the node's settings, and each connection's `TxSubmissionConfig`
  from its negotiated version.
- Feed the egress scheduler's credit: `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.FreshBytes`
  counts the bytes the chain freshly announces (an adopted block's size plus
  its announced endorser block's size, once and while fresh), and the kernel
  keeps the most one announcement can carry current from the ledger view.
