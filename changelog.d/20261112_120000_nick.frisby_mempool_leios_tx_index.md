### Breaking

- `LedgerSupportsMempool` has two new methods. `txLeiosMemoizedHashAndBytes`
  gives a transaction's memoized hash and its bytes as Leios sends and stores
  them. An endorser block references the transaction by that hash
  (`hashLeiosTx`) and the bytes' length. A block
  type without endorser blocks uses `nothingTxLeiosMemoizedHashAndBytes`.
  `tickedLedgerMajorProtocolVersionWord16` is
  the major protocol version, as a `VersionWord16`, under which a ticked
  ledger state validates transactions. A block type whose ledger never
  changes its protocol version gives that version to
  `fixedTickedLedgerMajorProtocolVersionWord16`.
- The `Mempool` has `getLeiosTxIndex`: the `ByteArray`s of its
  transactions that an endorser block can reference, by the hash it
  references them by, and the major protocol version they were all validated
  under, as a `LeiosMempoolTxs` from `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`.
  Callers restrict it to the requested hashes before converting the arrays to
  `ByteString`s outside STM.
