### Breaking

- `LedgerSupportsMempool` has two new methods, `txLeiosBytes` and
  `tickedLedgerProtVer`, for block types whose transactions endorser blocks
  can reference. A block type whose transactions they cannot reference uses
  `nothingTxLeiosBytes` and `nothingTickedLedgerProtVer`.
- The `Mempool` has `getLeiosTxIndex`: its transactions that an endorser block
  can reference, by the hash it references them by, and the protocol version
  they were validated under.
- `serializeTxForLeios` moves from `Ouroboros.Consensus.Shelley.Ledger.Forge`
  to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Shelley.Ledger.Mempool`.
