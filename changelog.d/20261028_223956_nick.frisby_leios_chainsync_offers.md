### Breaking

- The ChainSync client's `ConfigEnv` gains `leiosMsgRollForwardCallback`, for
  each valid roll-forward, and `leiosJumpAcceptedCallback`, for each ChainSync
  jump the peer accepts. `Handlers`' `hChainSyncClient` takes the peer's
  `LeiosFetchPeer`, if the version has the Leios mini-protocols.
- A peer that rolls forward a header containing a Leios certificate, or
  accepts a jump over one, offers the endorser block it certifies. A peer whose
  headers claim two endorser blocks certified for one election is disconnected
  (`ExnLeiosTwoCertificationClaims`).
