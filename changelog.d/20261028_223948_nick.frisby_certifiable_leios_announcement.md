### Breaking

- `LeiosProtocol` gains `getCertifiableLeiosAnnouncement`: the announcement
  that a header containing a Leios certificate certifies, given its
  predecessor's chain-dependent state. `defaultGetCertifiableLeiosAnnouncement`
  gives it for a protocol without Leios.
