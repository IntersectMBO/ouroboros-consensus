### Breaking

- `storeForgedEb` is removed, and the node kernel does not yet write the
  endorser blocks it forges to the LeiosDB.

### Non-Breaking

- The depth of the LeiosDB SQLite write queue counts two jobs for the forge:
  the body, which records its point, and the transactions.
