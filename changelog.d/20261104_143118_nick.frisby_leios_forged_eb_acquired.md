### Breaking

- `storeForgedEb` is removed. The node kernel hands each endorser block it
  forges, with the header announcing it, to LeiosFetch (`onForgedLeiosEb`),
  which takes them in as it would from a peer: it relays the announcement,
  writes the endorser block without the forge awaiting the writes, registers
  it and its transactions with the LeiosTxCache, never fetches it, and emits
  `AcquiredEb` and `AcquiredEbTxs` once both writes are durable, so the node
  offers it.
- `ForgedEndorserBlock` carries the parity of the protocol version its
  transactions were validated under (`forgedEbTxsParity`), which
  `forgeLeiosEb` takes.
- The function `forge` calls with a forged endorser block also takes the
  onset of the block's slot and the Leios part of the ledger view there.

### Non-Breaking

- LeiosFetch's `EbFetchState` has a new state, `ReceivingFromForge`, for a
  point whose endorser block the node's own forge produced and is writing. It
  overrides whatever the point was in (`markReceivingFromForge`), recording
  what of the point's acquisition the node had already emitted
  (`AlreadyEmitted`). LeiosFetch requests nothing at such a point, prunes
  every offer of it, and discards every reply for it, after validating it and
  releasing the request. Only the forge's writes settle it
  (`settleReceivingFromForge`), once both are durable, into `BodyAcquired`
  with no job left, emitting what had not been emitted yet.
- The depth of the LeiosDB SQLite write queue counts two jobs for the forge:
  the body, which records its point, and the transactions.
