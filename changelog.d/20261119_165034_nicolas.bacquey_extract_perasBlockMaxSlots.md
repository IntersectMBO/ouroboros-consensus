### Breaking

- Remove the `PerasBlockMaxSlots` parameter from `PerasParams`
- Replace the `PerasEnabled PerasRoundLength` parameter from `EraParams` with a ternary constructor including a `PerasBlockMaxSlots` value when it's relevant (i.e. in all Shelley eras, whether they are Peras-enabled or not)

### Non-Breaking

- Introduce helper functions to generate `PerasBlockMaxSlots` values.
- Set the value of `PerasBlockMaxSlots` to `k/f` in every Shelley-based era.
- Add two new queries to resolve the `PerasBlockMaxSlots` parameter from the era parameters, with a `SlotNo` or a `PerasRoundNo` input, respectively.
