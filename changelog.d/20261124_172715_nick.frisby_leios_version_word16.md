### Breaking

- The major protocol version that `forgeLeiosEb` takes and
  `forgedEbTxsProtVer` holds is a `VersionWord16` instead of a `Version`.

### Non-Breaking

- New in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsMempool`: `VersionWord16`, a
  major protocol version in 16 bits, and `toVersionWord16`, its conversion from
  a ledger `Version`.
