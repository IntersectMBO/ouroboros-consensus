### Breaking

- `withLeiosFetchPeer` passes its action the new `LeiosPeerLifetimeId` of the
  peer's registration, which keys the LeiosFetch decision loop's state. So a
  thread that outlives one registration of a peer never acts on a later one.
- `Handlers`' `hLeiosFetchClient` takes a `LeiosPeerLifetimeId`.
