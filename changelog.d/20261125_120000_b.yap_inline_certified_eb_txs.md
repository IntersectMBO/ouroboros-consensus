### Non-Breaking

- Add `inlineCertifiedEbTxs` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.ChainSync.Server`.
  It prepares a stored block for the node-to-client chain sync server: a block that certifies an endorser block gets the endorser block's transactions in its body, and other blocks are returned unchanged.
  It throws `CertRbWithoutEbTxs` if the transactions cannot be added. It is not used yet.
