### Breaking

- The Dijkstra `TxEbMeasure` is now `DijkstraEbMeasure`, and so is `HardForkTxEbMeasure` for the Cardano eras.
  It holds the block measure of the transactions an endorser block holds, and the size of their references in the endorser block.
  A Dijkstra transaction costs its block measure, and `encodeLeiosEbItemSize` of its byte size for its reference.
  A pre-Dijkstra transaction costs its block measure, and nothing for its reference.

- The Dijkstra `ebCapacityTxMeasure` also reads `maxEndorserBlockReferencesSize`, less the widest map header `encodeLeiosEb` writes.
  The Dijkstra `mempoolEbReservation` keeps only the block measure.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.EndorserBlock`, with the endorser-block encoder `encodeLeiosEb` and the sizes the mempool charges for it.
