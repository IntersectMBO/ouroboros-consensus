### Breaking

- ChainSel checks the Leios certificate of each arriving block against the
  Leios part of the ledger view at the block's slot. It records the claim that
  a valid certificate establishes, and an invalid certificate makes the block
  invalid.
- The ChainDB and `RunNode` require `BlockSupportsLeios`.
