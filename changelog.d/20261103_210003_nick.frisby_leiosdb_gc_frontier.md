### Non-Breaking

- The SQLite LeiosDB's garbage collection no longer marks the points it
  collects: its writer sweeps the unpinned points older than the latest slot
  it was given. That slot is not persisted, so after a restart, what an
  interrupted sweep left waits for the next garbage collection.
