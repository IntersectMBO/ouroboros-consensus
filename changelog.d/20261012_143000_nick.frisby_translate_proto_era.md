### Breaking

- `TranslateProto` gains a third parameter, the ledger era being translated
  into, and `translateLedgerView` takes that era's `TranslationContext`.
- The `TranslateProto` instances from `Praos` and from `TPraos` to `Praos2`
  require the era's `TranslationContext` to be `DijkstraGenesis`. A ledger view
  forecast across the Conway-to-Dijkstra boundary now has the Leios parameters
  of the Dijkstra genesis, instead of placeholder values.
