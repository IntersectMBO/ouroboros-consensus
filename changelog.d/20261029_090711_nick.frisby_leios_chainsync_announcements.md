### Non-Breaking

- The node accepts the announcement that a header carries once its ChainSync
  client has validated the header, as it would one from a LeiosNotify peer: it
  relays a new and fresh one, and focuses the election of a new one.
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosNotify.Client.Announcements.Validation`
  gains `chainSyncAnnouncement`.
