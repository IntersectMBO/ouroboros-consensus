### Non-Breaking

- The Dijkstra endorser-block capacity is zero while the epoch's committee
  cannot reach a quorum, that is while its seats with a voting key hold less
  stake than the quorum threshold. The mempool then makes no room for endorser
  blocks, and producers announce none.
