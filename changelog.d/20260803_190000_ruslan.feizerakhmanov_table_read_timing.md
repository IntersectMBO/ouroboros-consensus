### Non-Breaking

- `--benchmark-ledger-ops` now reports two additional `SlotDataPoint` fields, `tableReadTime` and `mut_tableRead`, timing the ledger-table fetch (`forkerReadTables`) that precedes a block's 5 timed ledger operations. Previously this fetch -- where an on-disk `LedgerDB` backend performs its UTxO-table reads -- fell entirely outside the window used to compute every other reported figure, including `totalTime`.
