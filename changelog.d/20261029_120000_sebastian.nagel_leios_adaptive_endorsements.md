### Breaking

- `LedgerSupportsMempool` gains `scaleEbCapacity` and `CanHardFork` gains
  `hardForkScaleEbCapacity`. Both default to the identity.
- `LeiosVoteState` gains `queryTally`.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gains `leiosAdaptiveEb` and `StdRunNodeArgs` gains
  `srnLeiosAdaptiveEb`.

### Non-Breaking

- A block producer can scale the endorser blocks it forges by how much of the
  stake voted in time for recent announcements (`LeiosAdaptiveEb`). It is off
  unless `srnLeiosAdaptiveEb` is set, and traces each sample as
  `LeiosAdaptiveEb`.
