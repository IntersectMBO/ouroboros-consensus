### Breaking

- The LeiosDB no longer emits what is written to it:
  `subscribeEbNotifications` is removed, and `LeiosEbNotification` moves to
  the new `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Acquisitions`. LeiosFetch emits its
  acquisitions there instead, on `LeiosAcquisitions`, a broadcast channel.
- The ChainDB's `ChainDbSpecificArgs` gains `cdbsSubscribeLeiosAcquisitions`,
  and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.openChainDB` takes the `LeiosAcquisitions`
  after the LeiosDB. Nothing is ever emitted on `noLeiosAcquisitions`.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gains `leiosAcquisitions`, and `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes the
  `LeiosAcquisitions` after the LeiosDB.
