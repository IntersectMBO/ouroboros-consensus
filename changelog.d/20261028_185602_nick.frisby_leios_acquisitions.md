### Breaking

- `LeiosAcquisitions`, a broadcast channel of the new
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Acquisitions`, carries each endorser block and
  closure the node acquires. The ChainDB subscribes to it, and keys its
  acquired closures by point.
- The ChainDB's `ChainDbSpecificArgs` gains `cdbsSubscribeLeiosAcquisitions`,
  and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.openChainDB` takes the `LeiosAcquisitions`
  after the LeiosDB. `noLeiosAcquisitions` subscribes to a broadcast on which
  nothing is ever emitted.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gains `leiosAcquisitions`, and `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes the
  `LeiosAcquisitions` after the LeiosDB.
