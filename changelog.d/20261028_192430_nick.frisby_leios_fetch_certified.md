### Breaking

- `ChainDbArgs` gains `cdbsOnNewLeiosValidClaim`, which ChainSel calls with
  the claim of each new Leios certification it validates, and the
  announcement it certifies. `completeChainDbArgs` takes it, and
  `ignoreNewLeiosValidClaim` is for what fetches nothing. LeiosFetch fetches
  the endorser block that announcement names, rather than the one its
  election's first announcement names.
- The new `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.NewValidClaims.NewLeiosValidClaims` holds
  those claims until the node kernel consumes them. `openChainDB` takes its
  `recordNewLeiosValidClaim`, and `mkNodeKernelArgs` its
  `consumeNewLeiosValidClaims`, which `NodeKernelArgs` gains as
  `nextNewLeiosValidClaims`.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Leios.isLeiosValidClaim` takes
  the whole `LeiosClaim`, rather than only its ranking block hash, and
  `insertLeiosValidClaim` gives `Nothing` when it records nothing.
