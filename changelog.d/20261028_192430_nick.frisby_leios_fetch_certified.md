### Breaking

- The ChainDB API gains `subscribeLeiosValidClaims`, the claim of each new
  Leios certification ChainSel validates, with the announcement it certifies.
  LeiosFetch fetches the endorser block that announcement names, rather than
  the one its election's first announcement names.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Leios.isLeiosValidClaim` takes
  the whole `LeiosClaim`, rather than only its ranking block hash, and
  `insertLeiosValidClaim` gives `Nothing` when it records nothing.
