### Breaking

- The LeiosFetch `MsgLeiosBlockTxsRequest` wire format changes: each bitmap
  chunk is now a CBOR byte string of up to 8 bytes (the CIP-164 MSB-first
  bitfield representation) instead of a 64-bit unsigned integer, and the
  bitmap's total width is the node's design ceiling on an endorser block's tx
  count, `maxTxsPerEndorserBlock` (currently 17142 txs, 268 chunks), rather
  than a protocol constant kept independent of it. Since that width need not
  be a multiple of 64, the last chunk (currently index 267) can be narrower
  than the others (currently 7 bytes instead of 8), with its trailing padding
  bits required to be clear. The decoder now rejects an empty request, a
  chunk index at or beyond that bound, a chunk of the wrong byte length, and
  an empty `MsgLeiosBlockTxs` reply. Old and new peers cannot interoperate on
  `MsgLeiosBlockTxsRequest`.
- In `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Type`, `TxBitmaps` is
  renamed to `TxsBitmap` and is now the new
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.SparseBitmap.SparseBitmap`; `bitmapToOffsets` and
  `offsetsToBitmap` give way to `txsBitmapToTxIndices` and
  `txsBitmapFromTxIndices maxTxs`, the latter now taking the bitmap's total
  width as an explicit `Int` argument; the module also exposes
  `txsBitmapChunkWidth`, `encodeTxsBitmap` and `decodeTxsBitmap maxTxs`.

### Non-Breaking

- New module `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.SparseBitmap`: a non-empty, chunked
  sparse bitmap over `Data.Bitmap` of cardano-strict-containers, with
  validated CBOR encode/decode and property tests. Requires the
  cardano-strict-containers that carries cardano-base PR #700 (`Data.Bitmap`),
  pinned in `cabal.project` until that PR lands.
