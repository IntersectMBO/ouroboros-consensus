### Breaking

- The LeiosFetch `MsgLeiosBlockTxsRequest` wire format changes: each bitmap
  chunk is now a CBOR byte string of 1 to 8 bytes (the CIP-164 MSB-first
  bitfield representation) instead of a 64-bit unsigned integer, taking only
  the bytes up to its last named tx. The decoder rejects an empty request, a
  zero chunk and a chunk past 64 bits, and an empty `MsgLeiosBlockTxs` reply.
  Old and new peers cannot interoperate on `MsgLeiosBlockTxsRequest`.
- In `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Type`, `TxBitmaps` is
  renamed to `TxsBitmap` and is now the new
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.SparseBitmap.SparseBitmap`; `bitmapToOffsets` and
  `offsetsToBitmap` give way to `txsBitmapToTxIndices` and
  `txsBitmapFromTxIndices`, both over a non-empty `NEIntSet`; the module also
  exposes `txsBitmapChunkWidth`, `encodeTxsBitmap` and `decodeTxsBitmap`.

### Non-Breaking

- New module `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.SparseBitmap`: a non-empty sparse
  bitmap of `Data.Bitmap 64` chunks from cardano-strict-containers. Requires
  the cardano-strict-containers that carries cardano-base PR #700
  (`Data.Bitmap`).
