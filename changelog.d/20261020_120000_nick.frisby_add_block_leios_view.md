### Breaking

- The ChainDB's `addBlockAsync`, `addBlock`, `addBlock_` and
  `addBlockWaitWrittenToDisk` take the slot of the block's predecessor, the
  Leios part of the ledger view at the block's slot, and the announcement that
  the block's Leios certificate certifies, if any.
- BlockFetch's matched block is now a `MatchedBlock`, which carries those
  three from the header the block matched.
- `InitChainDB`'s `addBlock` is renamed `addTheFirstEbb`.
- `ChainDB.fromChain` requires a block whose ledger view is trivial.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol` gains `trivialLeiosLedgerView`.
