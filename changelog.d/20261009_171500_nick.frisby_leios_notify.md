### Breaking

- `Codecs`, `Apps`, `ByteLimits` and `Handlers` in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode` gain fields for the LeiosNotify
  mini-protocol, which runs as a hot mini-protocol on `NodeToNodeV_16` and
  later.
- `RunNode` has the new superclass `LeiosProtocol (BlockProtocol blk)`, from
  the new module `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol`.
- `NodeKernel` gains `getLeiosNotifyClientStates`. `NodeKernelArgs` gains
  `maxClockSkew`, which `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes in place of a
  `SomeHeaderInFutureCheck`.
- `PolyPraosLedgerView`'s Leios fields become the one field `plvLeios`, a
  `LeiosLedgerView`, and `AnnouncedBy` gives way to `LeiosAnnouncement`.

### Non-Breaking

- New LeiosNotify mini-protocol, in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosNotify.*`, numbered as the
  cardano-blueprint numbers it.
- The node's LeiosNotify client validates each announcement, offer and vote,
  and disconnects a peer that sends one an honest peer would not.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.CBOR` gains `decodeListUpTo` and `decodeMapUpTo`.
- Requires `typed-protocols` 1.2.2.
