### Breaking

- `Codecs`, `Apps` and `ByteLimits` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode`
  take a new type parameter, after the Peras ones, for the LeiosNotify
  mini-protocol, and gain its fields `cLeiosNotifyCodec`, `aLeiosNotifyClient`,
  `aLeiosNotifyServer` and `blLeiosNotify`. `Handlers` gains
  `hLeiosNotifyClient` and `hLeiosNotifyServer`. `initiator` and
  `initiatorAndResponder` run LeiosNotify, as a hot mini-protocol, on
  `NodeToNodeV_16` and later.

### Non-Breaking

- New LeiosNotify mini-protocol, in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosNotify.{Type,Client,Server}`, with
  `MsgQuit` for graceful shutdown. Its server never has a notification to send,
  and answers each outstanding request with `MsgCanceled` once the client quits.
  Its client keeps its requests pipelined, discards the replies, and disconnects
  a peer that cancels a request before the client has quit. It sends no request
  until the immutable tip can forecast to the current slot
  (`awaitImmTipCanForecastNow`).
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.LeiosNotify` gives LeiosNotify's mini-protocol
  number and pipelining bound, and runs a lookahead peer with limits.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `encodeLeiosPoint`,
  `decodeLeiosPoint` and a `LeiosVote` with its codec.
- Requires `typed-protocols` 1.2.2, for its lookahead peers.
