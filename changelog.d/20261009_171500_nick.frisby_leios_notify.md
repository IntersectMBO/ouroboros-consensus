### Breaking

- `Codecs`, `Apps` and `ByteLimits` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode`
  take a new type parameter, after the Peras ones, for the LeiosNotify
  mini-protocol, and gain its fields `cLeiosNotifyCodec`, `aLeiosNotifyClient`,
  `aLeiosNotifyServer` and `blLeiosNotify`. `Handlers` gains
  `hLeiosNotifyClient` and `hLeiosNotifyServer`. `initiator` and
  `initiatorAndResponder` run LeiosNotify, as a hot mini-protocol, on
  `NodeToNodeV_16` and later.
- `RunNode` has the new superclass `LeiosProtocol (BlockProtocol blk)`, from the
  new module `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol`: what a protocol says about
  the Leios announcement a header carries, how to validate one out of context,
  and the Leios parameters its ledger view carries, as a `LeiosLedgerView`.
  Every protocol in this repository has an instance; only `Praos2`'s headers
  carry announcements. The `RunNode` instance of `HardForkBlock xs` additionally
  requires `All SingleEraBlockAndLeiosProtocol xs`, and `mkHandlers` requires
  `LeiosProtocol (BlockProtocol blk)`, `ConvertRawHash blk` and
  `HasHardForkHistory blk`.
- `hLeiosNotifyClient` takes the client's state variable, and `NodeKernel` gains
  `getLeiosNotifyClientStates`, which a new node-kernel thread prunes as the
  immutable tip advances.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gains `maxClockSkew`, and `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes it in place
  of the `SomeHeaderInFutureCheck`, which it builds from it.

### Non-Breaking

- New LeiosNotify mini-protocol, in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosNotify.{Type,Client,Server}`, with
  `MsgQuit` for graceful shutdown. Its server never has a notification to send,
  and answers each outstanding request with `MsgCanceled` once the client quits.
  Its client keeps its requests pipelined, passes each reply to the node's
  checks, and disconnects a peer that cancels a request before the client has
  quit. It sends no request until the immutable tip can forecast to the current
  slot (`awaitImmTipCanForecastNow`). `MsgLeiosVotes` carries at most
  `maxLeiosVotesPerMessage` votes, sixteen; the codec rejects more. A reply may
  take at most `maxLeiosNotifyReplyBytes`, 1.5 KiB, which with the pipelining
  bound sets LeiosNotify's ingress queue.
- The node's LeiosNotify client fully validates each `MsgLeiosBlockAnnouncement`
  (`Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosNotify.Client.{Announcements,State,Validate}`):
  the header must carry an announcement, a peer may announce at most twice per
  election and never repeat itself, the in-future check and an age bound apply,
  and the header gets the protocol-level validation, against the immutable tip
  forecast to its slot. It disconnects the peer otherwise. Nothing else in the
  node processes the announcement yet.
- It also checks each `MsgLeiosBlockOffer`: a peer may offer only an endorser
  block it announced itself, only once, not below the immutable tip, and with a
  size between one byte and the ledger view's bound for that slot.
- And each `MsgLeiosBlockTxsOffer`: a peer may closure-offer only an endorser
  block it announced itself, only once, and not below the immutable tip.
- And each `MsgLeiosVotes`: it may not be empty, each vote must be for an
  announcement the peer sent, a seat may vote once per slot, and a vote must
  arrive between 3 L_hdr and 3 L_hdr + L_vote after its election slot began,
  give or take the node's maximum clock skew and `voteLateGrace`. It does not
  check a vote's seat or signature: the node's central vote processing will,
  once per vote, caching the result.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Protocol.PolyPraos` gains `doValidateKESSignatureWorker`
  and `WhetherToUpperBoundOCERT`, and the announcement helpers
  `leiosAnnouncementPolyPraos`, `getLeiosLedgerViewPolyPraos` and
  `validateLeiosAnnouncementPolyPraos`.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.LeiosNotify` gives LeiosNotify's mini-protocol
  number and pipelining bound, and runs a lookahead peer with limits.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `ElectionId`, `encodeLeiosPoint`,
  `decodeLeiosPoint` and a `LeiosVote` with its codec.
- Requires `typed-protocols` 1.2.2, for its lookahead peers.
