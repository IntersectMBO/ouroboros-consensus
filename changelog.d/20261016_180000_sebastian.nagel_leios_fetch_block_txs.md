### Breaking

- `MsgLeiosBlockTxs` carries only the transactions, `[4, [* tx]]`, as in
  ouroboros-consensus#2416. The client checks them against its own request
  instead of the echoed point and bitmaps. The decoder accepts a definite or
  indefinite list of at most `maxTxsPerEb` transactions.
- A LeiosFetch transaction is CBOR-in-CBOR, `#6.24(bytes .cbor block_transaction)`,
  as in ouroboros-consensus#2416, where it was a plain byte string.
