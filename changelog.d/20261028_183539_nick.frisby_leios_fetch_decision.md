### Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Type.bitmapOffsets` is renamed
  `bitmapToOffsets`.

### Non-Breaking

- New `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Decision`, which decides
  for all peers at once what to request of the endorser blocks the node is
  acquiring, from the peers' offers and within a byte budget per peer.
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Jobs` divides a closure into
  the jobs it requests.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `LeiosEbByteSizeBounds`, the ledger's
  bounds on the byte sizes of an endorser block.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Type` gains `offsetsToBitmap`,
  the inverse of `bitmapToOffsets`.
