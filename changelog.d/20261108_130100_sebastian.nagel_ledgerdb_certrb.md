### Breaking

- The LedgerDB applies a block containing a Leios certificate on its predecessor's state after the closure of the endorser block it certifies, when validating, replaying and in `reapplyThenPushNOW`.
  `LedgerDB.openDB`, `V2.mkInitDb`, `ValidateArgs` and `V2.reapplyThenPush` take a `ResolveLeiosClosure`, which the ChainDB reads from the LeiosDB.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `resolveLeiosClosure` and `LeiosClosureMissing` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LeiosDB.API`, and `applyWithLeiosClosure` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LedgerDB.Forker`.
