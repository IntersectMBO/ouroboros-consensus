### Breaking

- The LedgerDB applies a block containing a Leios certificate on its predecessor's state after the endorser block it certifies, when validating, replaying and in `reapplyThenPushNOW`.
  `LedgerDB.openDB`, `V2.mkInitDb`, `ValidateArgs` and `V2.reapplyThenPush` take a `ResolveEndorserBlock`, which the ChainDB reads from the LeiosDB.

- `ChainDbFailure` gains `ChainDbMissingEndorserBlock` and `ChainDbUndecodableEndorserBlock`.
  Like a missing block, they shut the node down and trigger full validation on the next start.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `lookupLeiosClosure` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LeiosDB.API`, `getKnownEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Query`, and `ResolveEndorserBlock` and `applyWithEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LedgerDB.Forker`.
