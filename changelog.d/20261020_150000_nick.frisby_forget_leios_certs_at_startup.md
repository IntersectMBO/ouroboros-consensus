### Breaking

- The VolatileDB gains `forgetLeiosCertsAtStartUpExcept`, and its trace gains
  `ForgotLeiosCertBlocks` and `ReadmittedForgottenBlock`. After initial chain
  selection, the ChainDB forgets each block containing a Leios certificate that
  is not on the selection, until the block is added again, so that ChainSel
  checks its certificate when it arrives. Forgetting stands in for deleting,
  which the VolatileDB's append-only files cannot do; see
  `docs/website/contents/explanations/valid_claims_startup.md`.
