### Breaking

- The LeiosDB's writes no longer report which closures they complete:
  `CompletedEbs` is removed, `writeEbBody`'s promise gives only the offsets
  at which the point holds its transactions, and `writeTxs`'s gives the
  offsets of the transactions it dropped because the endorser block referenced
  them with the wrong size. LeiosFetch disconnects a peer that serves such
  transactions (`ExnLeiosBlockTxsWrongSize`), and acquires no closure from
  them.
