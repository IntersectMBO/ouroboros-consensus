### Breaking

- `maxAnnouncementAgeSend` and `maxAnnouncementAgeRecv` become the accessors
  of `LeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`, and `defaultLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits` has
  their former values. `mkLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits` builds other limits, and
  rejects (`InvalidLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`) a negative send limit or a
  receive limit that does not exceed it. `StdRunNodeArgs`'
  `srnLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits` overrides the default in `NodeKernelArgs`'
  `leiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`. A network whose immutable tip is only minutes
  old, such as a test network, needs smaller limits.
- `newAnnounceState` and `announcementValidity` take the limits, and
  `AnnounceState` exports them as `ageLimits`.
- `leiosVoteWindow` no longer ends a vote's window at `maxAnnouncementAgeSend`,
  which it never did on mainnet.
