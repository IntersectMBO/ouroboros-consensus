### Breaking

- `maxAnnouncementAgeSend` and `maxAnnouncementAgeRecv` become the fields of
  `LeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`, and `defaultLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits` has
  their former values. `StdRunNodeArgs`' `srnLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`
  overrides that default in `NodeKernelArgs`' `leiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`,
  and `NodeKernel` gains `getLeiosAnnouncementAgeLimits`. A network whose
  immutable tip is only minutes old, such as a test network, needs smaller
  limits.
- `newAnnounceState`, `announcementValidity`, `onLeiosNotifyAnnouncement`,
  `checkLeiosVotes`, `leiosVoteWindow` and `leiosNotifyClient` take the
  limits.
