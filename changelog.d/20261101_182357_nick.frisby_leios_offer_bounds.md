### Breaking

- `ValidAnnouncement` gains `announcementEbBounds`, the ledger's bounds on
  the endorser block as of the announcement's slot. `validateAnnouncementHeader`
  also returns them, and `chainSyncAnnouncement` and the ChainSync client's
  `leiosMsgRollForwardCallback` take the Leios part of the ledger view at the
  header's slot.
- `AcquiredEb` carries the size of the endorser block's body.
- `LeiosEbBounds` moves to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`, beside the new
  `LeiosEbSizes`, `leiosEbSizes` and `admitsSizes`, and `leiosEbBounds` to
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol`.
