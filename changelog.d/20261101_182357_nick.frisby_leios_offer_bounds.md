### Breaking

- The LeiosNotify relay offers an acquired endorser block only at the
  announcements whose bounds admit it. `NotifyAnnouncement` carries those
  bounds, and `ValidAnnouncement` gains `announcementEbBounds`.
  `validateAnnouncementHeader` also returns them, and `chainSyncAnnouncement`
  and the ChainSync client's `leiosMsgRollForwardCallback` take the Leios part
  of the ledger view at the header's slot.
- `AcquiredEb` and `AcquiredEbTxs` carry the endorser block's `LeiosEbSizes`.
- `LeiosEbBounds` moves to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`, beside the new
  `LeiosEbSizes`, `leiosEbSizes` and `admitsSizes`, and `leiosEbBounds` to
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol`.
