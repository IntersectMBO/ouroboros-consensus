### Breaking

- `ValidAnnouncement` gains `announcementEbBounds`, the ledger's bounds on
  the endorser block as of the announcement's slot. `validateAnnouncementHeader`
  also returns them.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `LeiosEbSizes`, `leiosEbSizes` and
  `admitsSizes`, and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol` gains
  `leiosEbBounds`.
