### Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types.LeiosEbByteSizeBounds` becomes
  `LeiosEbByteSizes SizeLimit`. `LeiosEbByteSizes` holds an endorser block's
  body size (`lebBodySize`) and the total size of the transactions it
  references (`lebTxsSize`, now a `Word64`), and its `SizeRole` index says
  whether these are the ledger's limits or a block's actual sizes.
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Decision` no longer re-exports
  it.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `leiosEbByteSizes`, an endorser
  block's actual sizes, and `idLeiosEbByteSizesLimit`, which fixes the index
  of a `LeiosEbByteSizes` of limits where nothing else does.
