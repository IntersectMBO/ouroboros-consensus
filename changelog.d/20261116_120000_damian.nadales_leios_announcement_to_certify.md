### Breaking

- `LeiosProtocol` gains `leiosAnnouncementToCertify`: the announcement that a
  header at a given slot may certify, or why it may certify none, given its
  predecessor's chain-dependent state ticked to that slot. Header validation
  rejects a header that contains a Leios certificate exactly when it gives
  `Left`. `defaultLeiosAnnouncementToCertify` gives it for a protocol without
  Leios.

### Non-Breaking

- `WhyNotCertifiable` moves to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol`.
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Protocol.PolyPraos` still exports it.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol` exports `NotCertifiable`, the reason
  that `leiosAnnouncementToCertify` gives.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Protocol.PolyPraos` exports `certifiablePolyPraos`,
  the rule that `leiosHeaderChecks` applies, and `leiosHeaderChecks` for tests.
