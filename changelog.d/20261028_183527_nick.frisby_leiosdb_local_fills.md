### Breaking

- The LeiosDB's `writeEbBody` takes local fills (`LocalFill`): offsets of the
  new body whose transactions another point durably holds. It copies
  those bytes as it writes the body, and its promise gives the offsets at
  which the point then holds its transactions, filled by this write or an
  earlier one.
