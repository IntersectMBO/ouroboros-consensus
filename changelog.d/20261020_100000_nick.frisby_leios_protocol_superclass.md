### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsProtocol` requires `LeiosProtocol` of the block's protocol, and
  `ShelleyProtocol` requires `LeiosProtocol`. `RunNode` and the ChainDB no
  longer list it separately.
- The hard fork combinator's `SingleEraBlockAndLeiosProtocol` is removed, since
  `SingleEraBlock` now implies it.
