### Breaking

- A block that certifies an endorser block also announces one, of the
  transactions that apply after the certified closure.
- `ValidatedTxWithDiffs` moved to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Mempool.API`.
- `Mempool` has a new field, `getTxsAfter`.
- `TraceForgeEvent` has new constructors: `TraceForgeFromView`,
  `TraceForgeViewPartial` and `TraceForgeNoView`.
- `forge` takes the forge views to read.

### Non-Breaking

- A block producer prepares forge views one second before each slot it leads:
  the mempool's transactions re-applied on its tip ticked to that slot, and on
  the tip with the closure of the endorser block it announced. The forge splits
  the matching view instead of re-applying the mempool, and warns when the view
  was not ready in time.
- Added `Ouroboros.Consensus.Mempool.ForgeView`,
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.NodeKernel.ForgeView` and `partitionTxSeq`.
