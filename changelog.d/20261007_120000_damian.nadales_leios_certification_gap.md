### Non-Breaking

- New `certificationGapElapsed` and `LeiosPeriods` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`.
  `certificationGapElapsed` says whether a ranking block can certify the endorser block that its parent announced.
  It follows CIP-0164, Step 5, rule 3.
  The ranking block must be at least `ceiling ((3 * L_hdr + L_vote + L_diff) / slotLength)` slots after its parent.
  A ranking block exactly that many slots after its parent can certify.
  Block forging and block validation do not call it yet.
