### Breaking

- `AcceptedVote` gains `voteQuorum`, the weight of the votes that certify an
  announcement in its election slot.
- `SlotVotes` gains `slotWeights`, the weight of the votes for each
  announcement. `recordVote` returns the votes' signatures when they reach
  quorum, and `pruneVotes` returns the votes it forgets.
- `leiosNotifyPruner` takes the node's `VoteState`.
- `TraceLeiosVoteState` gains `TraceLeiosCertified`,
  `TraceLeiosAggregationFailed` and `TraceLeiosQuorumMissed`.

### Non-Breaking

- The node aggregates the votes for an announcement into its certificate once
  they reach quorum by their deadline, and keeps the certificate until the
  announcing block is immutable (`getCertificate`, `pruneCertificates`). It
  traces an announcement whose votes missed quorum by their deadline.
