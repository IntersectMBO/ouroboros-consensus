### Breaking

- `AcceptedVote` gains `acceptedQuorum`, the weight of the votes that certify an
  announcement in its election slot.
- `SlotVotes` gains `slotTallies`, the weight of the votes for each
  announcement. `recordVote` returns the votes' signatures when they reach
  quorum, and `pruneVotes` traces the announcements whose votes missed quorum,
  so it runs in its own transaction.
- `votePruner` and `notifyLogPruner` are gone: the node prunes the notify log
  and the votes in one thread, with `pruneNotifyLog` and `pruneVotes`, every
  `leiosDeadlinePrunerInterval`, which replaces `notifyLogPruneCadence`.
- `leiosNotifyPruner` takes the node's `VoteState`.
- `ExnLeiosInvalidVote` carries the vote and its election slot rather than the
  `AcceptedVote`.
- `TraceLeiosVoteState` gains `TraceLeiosCertified`,
  `TraceLeiosAggregationFailed` and `TraceLeiosQuorumMissed`.

### Non-Breaking

- The node aggregates the votes for an announcement into its certificate once
  they reach quorum by their deadline, and keeps the certificate until the
  announcing block is immutable (`getCertificate`, `pruneCertificates`). It
  traces an announcement whose votes missed quorum by their deadline.
