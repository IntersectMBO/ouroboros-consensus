### Breaking

- `lookupEbBody` returns the EB body in its envelope, in the bytes it arrived in, as `Maybe ByteString`, instead of its decoded tx hashes and sizes.
  Missing bodies are `Nothing` instead of an empty list.

- The LeiosFetch server sends the endorser block bytes it stores without decoding them.
  Its `BlockServed` trace gives the endorser block's size in bytes instead of its number of transactions.

- LeiosDB stores an EB's body in its envelope, in the bytes it arrived in, in a new `ebBodies` table.
  There is no migration: delete existing LeiosDB files.
