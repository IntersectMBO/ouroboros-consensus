### Breaking

- An endorser block may not be certified across an epoch boundary.
  `tickChainDepStatePolyPraos` clears the pending announcement when it ticks
  into a new epoch, so a header that certifies across one fails with
  `LeiosCertWithoutAnnouncement`. It now requires `Functor (WhenLeios proto)`.
- `ResolveLeiosBlock` gains `tickedProtocolStateLeiosAnnouncement`: the
  announcement a header may certify, read from the chain-dep state ticked to
  its slot. `decideLeiosCertify` takes that ticked state and reads it, so the
  forge does not certify across an epoch boundary either.
