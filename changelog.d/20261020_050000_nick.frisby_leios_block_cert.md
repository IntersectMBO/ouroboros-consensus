### Breaking

- `SingleEraBlock` requires the new `BlockSupportsLeios`, whose
  `blockLeiosCert` gives the Leios certificate a block contains.
- `ShelleyBasedEra`'s `blockBodyContainsLeiosCert` is replaced by
  `blockBodyLeiosCert`, which gives the certificate itself.
- `ExtValidationError` gains `ExtValidationErrorLeiosCert`, for a block whose
  Leios certificate does not establish its claim.

### Non-Breaking

- The new `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Cert` gives the claim a block's Leios
  certificate makes (`leiosClaim`) and verifies the certificate against the
  committee in the ledger view at the block's slot (`verifyLeiosClaim`).
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Leios` gains `LeiosValidClaims`,
  the claims some valid certificate has established.
