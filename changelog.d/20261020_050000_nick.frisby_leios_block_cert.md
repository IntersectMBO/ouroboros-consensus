### Breaking

- `SingleEraBlock` requires the new `BlockSupportsLeios`, whose
  `blockLeiosCert` gives the Leios certificate a block contains.
- `ShelleyBasedEra`'s `blockBodyContainsLeiosCert` is replaced by
  `blockBodyLeiosCert`, which gives the certificate itself.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Leios` gains
  `verifyBlockLeiosCert`, which verifies a block's Leios certificate against
  the committee in the ledger view at the block's slot.
