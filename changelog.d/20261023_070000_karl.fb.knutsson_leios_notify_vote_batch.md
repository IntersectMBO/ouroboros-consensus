### Non-Breaking

- The LeiosNotify server lets a vote join the votes message at the tail of
  the peer's outgoing queue when that has room, instead of starting a
  message of its own, so a peer whose queue is backed up gets fewer, fuller
  messages and loses fewer votes to exhausted credits. Nothing waits for a
  batch to fill. The cap, `leiosNotifyVoteBatch`, is derived from the
  receiver's ingress queue, the pipeline depth and the vote size; the codec
  refuses a `MsgLeiosVotes` with no votes or more than
  `leiosNotifyMaxVotesPerMessage`.
