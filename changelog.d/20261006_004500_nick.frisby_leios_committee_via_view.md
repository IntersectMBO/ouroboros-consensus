### Breaking

- `HasLeiosVoting` loses `getLeiosCommittee` and `getCurrentThreshold`. Use `leiosCommitteeOfTip` from `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsProtocol`, which applies `getLeiosCommitteeFromView` to `ledgerViewOfTip`, so the state-derived and the view-derived committee are one definition.

- `getLeiosCommitteeFromView` and `getMinCertificationGap` default to `Nothing`, so eras without Leios declare an empty instance.

- `LeiosMissingThreshold` is removed: the committee and its quorum threshold now come together, so a committee without a threshold cannot be observed.
