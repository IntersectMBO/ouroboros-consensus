### Patch

- `getLeiosCommittee` and `getCurrentThreshold` now read through `getLeiosCommitteeFromView`, applied to the ledger view the state projects at its own tip slot, rather than each reaching into the state itself. Previously three definitions knew that the committee lives at `ssStakeSet . ssLeiosCommittee` and the quorum at `ppLeiosQuorumStakeThreshold`; now one does, so the view-derived accessor and the state-derived ones agree by construction instead of by copies staying in step. That the two agreed was already asserted in a comment; it is now structural.

- The projection is `SL.currentForecast`, which is `SL.mkForecast` --- no TICKF --- so the state-derived accessors cost what they did before. `protocolLedgerView` and the anchor-slot branch of `ledgerViewForecastAt` are defined by the same projection for the same reason, which is what this extends to the Leios accessors.

- No signature changed: `HasLeiosVoting` keeps all three methods, the two new helpers are module-private, and nothing downstream moved.
