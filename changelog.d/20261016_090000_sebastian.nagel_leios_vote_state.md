### Breaking

- `NodeKernel` gains `getLeiosVoteState`.
- `checkLeiosVotes` also returns the `AcceptedVote`s: each vote with its
  election slot, its committee and its `VoteRelayWindow` (`leiosVoteRelayWindow`).
- `leiosNotifyClient` takes the node's `VoteState`, and a tracer of
  `TraceLeiosNotifyClientEvent`s.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosNotifyClientTracer`, which traces each
  vote the node adds, once, rather than each copy that arrives.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosVoteStateTracer`, of
  `TraceLeiosVoteState`s, which `newVoteState` takes.
- A `NotifyEntry` is a `Notification` with until when it is worth relaying,
  and the log prunes each once that has passed. `Notification` has
  `NotifyAnnouncement` and `NotifyVote`.
- `ExnLeiosNotifyInvalidVotes` gains `ExnLeiosInvalidVote`.

### Non-Breaking

- The node relays the votes its LeiosNotify clients accept: the first valid
  vote of each seat in an election, only for an announcement the node relayed,
  and only from when the vote's window opens until its diffusion period ends
  or that announcement expires.
- The node disconnects a peer that sends an invalid vote, unless it already
  has a vote of that seat in that election.
- The node's client accepts a vote until `voteLateGrace` after its diffusion
  period ends, rather than after its voting period ends.
- New `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.VoteState`, the votes the node has accepted
  from any peer, for every election whose votes are still worth relaying. Its
  `voteRelay` thread, which the node runs, relays a vote accepted before its
  window opened once it opens.
- `AnnounceState` tells whether the node accepted an announcement
  (`hasAnnouncement`).
- `...Client.Announcements.Validation` exports `announcingRbHash`.
- Pruning the `NotifyLog` costs time in proportion to what expires, not to the
  size of the log.
