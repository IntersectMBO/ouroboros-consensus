### Non-Breaking

- New `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache`, the LeiosTxCache: an in-memory
  index of the transactions that the most recent endorser block announcements
  reference, by reference count. It records whether each transaction is
  acquired or validated, and where its durable bytes are.
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache.API` is its interface, and
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache.Reference` is its pure reference
  implementation, which `newPureLeiosTxCache` wraps.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `TxLocation`.
