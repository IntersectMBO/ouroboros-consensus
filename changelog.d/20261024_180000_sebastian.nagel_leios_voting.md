### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `decodeEbTx`, with which a voter decodes the
  transactions of a closure one by one, and `ebTxsProtVerParity` and
  `assumeValidatedEbTx`, with which it reapplies a transaction validated
  before. Block types without endorser blocks use `defaultDecodeEbTx`,
  `defaultEbTxsProtVerParity` and `defaultAssumeValidatedEbTx`.
- `LeiosProtocol` gains `leiosBLSKeys`, the BLS keys of a protocol's
  credentials to lead.
- `CanBeLeader (Praos2 c)` is `Praos2CanBeLeader c`: Praos's credentials and
  the BLS key, if any.
- `ShelleyLeaderCredentials` gains `shelleyLeaderCredentialsBLSKey`.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosVotingTracer`, of `TraceLeiosVoting`s.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gain `leiosVoterTxCache`, which transactions the node
  validated already. `mkNodeKernelArgs` knows of none yet.
- `TxEbMeasureByteSize` gains `txEbMeasureTxsByteSize`, the bytes the
  transactions of an endorser block may take.

### Non-Breaking

- The node votes for each endorser block whose closure it acquires, with each
  BLS key of its block forging that holds a seat in the election: if the tip
  of its selection announced the block, the closure applies to the tip's
  ledger state, and, once the window to vote opens, the node saw no other
  announcement of the election and the window has not closed.
- The node validates a closure as soon as it is complete, and each endorser
  block in a thread of its own.
- The node votes only for an endorser block whose transactions fit
  `ebCapacityTxMeasure`, by what the block claims of their sizes and by what
  they take once decoded.
- `AnnounceState` tells whether the node accepted another announcement of an
  election (`isEquivocated`).
- `AnnounceState` tells how long after its election slot began the node
  accepted an announcement (`acceptedAnnouncementAge`). The node votes only for
  an announcement it accepted within L_hdr.
