### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `validateEndorserBlock`, with which a voter
  validates an endorser block on the ledger state of the announcing block,
  reading the values it needs and looking up and recording transactions in
  the tx cache through the given functions. It
  yields a `ValidationSummary`, or an `InvalidEndorserBlock` packing the
  block type's reason. Block types without endorser blocks use
  `defaultValidateEndorserBlock`.
- The `BlockSupportsLeios` instance of `ShelleyBlock` moves to
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Shelley.Leios`.
- `LeiosProtocol` gains `getLeiosKeys`, the BLS keys of a protocol's
  credentials to lead.
- `CanBeLeader` of `Praos c` and `Praos2 c` is `PolyPraosCanBeLeader`:
  Praos's credentials and, with Leios, the BLS key, if any.
- `ShelleyLeaderCredentials` gains `shelleyLeaderCredentialsBLSKey`.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosVotingTracer`, of `TraceLeiosVoting`s.

### Non-Breaking

- The node votes for the endorser block the tip of its selection announces,
  once it acquired all of it, with each BLS key of its block forging that
  holds a seat in the election: if it accepted the announcement within L_hdr
  and no other of the election, and its transactions apply to the announcing
  block's ledger state. It votes once the window to vote opens.
- The node validates one endorser block at a time, as soon as it is complete, and
  stops once the tip announces another block or the window closes. The
  transactions it validated stay in the LeiosTxCache, so validating again
  only reapplies them.
- The node never votes for competing blocks: it targets no block older than
  its last target, and votes at most once per election.
- The node votes only for an endorser block whose transactions fit
  `ebCapacityTxMeasure`, as when forging.
- `AnnounceState` tells whether the node accepted another announcement of the
  election of an announcing block (`isEquivocated`), and how long after its
  election slot began it accepted the block's (`acceptedAnnouncementAge`).
