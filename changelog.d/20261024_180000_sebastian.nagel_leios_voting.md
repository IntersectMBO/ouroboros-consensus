### Breaking

- `RunNode` requires the new `LedgerSupportsEndorserBlocks`, with which a
  voter applies the closure of an endorser block. Its default decodes no
  transaction; Shelley-based eras decode one as a transaction of a block of
  the era.
- `LeiosProtocol` gains `leiosBLSKeys`, the BLS keys of a protocol's
  credentials to lead.
- `CanBeLeader (Praos2 c)` is `Praos2CanBeLeader c`: Praos's credentials and
  the BLS key, if any.
- `ShelleyLeaderCredentials` gains `shelleyLeaderCredentialsBLSKey`.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosVotingTracer`, of `TraceLeiosVoting`s.

### Non-Breaking

- The node votes for each endorser block whose closure it acquires, with each
  BLS key of its block forging that holds a seat in the election: if the tip
  of its selection announced the block, the closure applies to the tip's
  ledger state, and, once the window to vote opens, the node saw no other
  announcement of the election and the window has not closed.
- The node validates a closure as soon as it is complete, and each endorser
  block in a thread of its own.
- `AnnounceState` tells whether the node accepted another announcement of an
  election (`isEquivocated`).
