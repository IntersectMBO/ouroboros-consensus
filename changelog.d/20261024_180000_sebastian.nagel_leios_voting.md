### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `validateLeiosClosure`, with which a voter
  validates a closure on the ledger state of the announcing block, reading
  the values it needs and the transactions validated before, and recording
  each it validates in full, through the given functions. It yields a
  `LeiosClosureValidation`. Block types without
  endorser blocks use `defaultValidateLeiosClosure`; others can use
  `validateClosureInMempool` from `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.ClosureValidation`,
  which validates through the mempool's `applyTx` and `reapplyTx`.
- `LeiosProtocol` gains `leiosBLSKeys`, the BLS keys of a protocol's
  credentials to lead.
- `CanBeLeader (Praos2 c)` is `Praos2CanBeLeader c`: Praos's credentials and
  the BLS key, if any.
- `ShelleyLeaderCredentials` gains `shelleyLeaderCredentialsBLSKey`.
- The node-kernel `Tracers` gain `leiosVotingTracer`, of `TraceLeiosVoting`s.
- `TxEbMeasureByteSize` gains `txEbMeasureTxsByteSize`, the bytes the
  transactions of an endorser block may take.

### Non-Breaking

- The node votes for the endorser block the tip of its selection announces,
  once it acquired its closure, with each BLS key of its block forging that
  holds a seat in the election: if it accepted the announcement within L_hdr
  and no other of the election, and the closure applies to the announcing
  block's ledger state. It votes once the window to vote opens.
- The node validates one closure at a time, as soon as it is complete, and
  stops once the tip announces another block or the window closes. The
  transactions it validated stay in the LeiosTxCache, so validating again
  only reapplies them.
- The node never votes for competing blocks: it targets no block older than
  its last target, and votes at most once per election.
- The node votes only for an endorser block whose transactions fit
  `ebCapacityTxMeasure`, by what the block claims of their sizes and by what
  they take once decoded.
- `AnnounceState` tells whether the node accepted another announcement of the
  election of an announcing block (`isEquivocated`), and how long after its
  election slot began it accepted the block's (`acceptedAnnouncementAge`).
