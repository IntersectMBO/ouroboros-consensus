### Non-Breaking

- New pipelined LeiosFetch client (`leiosFetchClientPeerPipelined`), with
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Client.Replies`: it sends the
  requests assigned to its peer, checks and writes the replies, and emits
  each acquisition once its write is durable. A peer that replies as an honest
  one would not is disconnected by an `ExnLeiosFetchInvalidReply`. If
  transactions with the wrong sizes reach the LeiosDB, which drops them,
  LeiosFetch disconnects the peer that served them (`ExnLeiosBlockTxsWrongSize`)
  and fetches no more of that endorser block's closure at that point, since no
  peer can complete it.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Trace` gains
  `TraceLeiosFetchClientEvent`, `FetchArrivalBytes` and
  `LeiosTxCacheInsertBodySummary`, which
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache.API` re-exports.
