### Non-Breaking

- New pipelined LeiosFetch client (`leiosFetchClientPeerPipelined`), with
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Client.Replies`: it sends the
  requests assigned to its peer, checks and writes the replies, and announces
  each acquisition once its write is durable. A peer that replies as an honest
  one would not is disconnected by an `ExnLeiosFetchInvalidReply`.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Trace` gains
  `TraceLeiosFetchClientEvent`.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `hashLeiosTx`, and
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache.API` gains `PackedEndorserBlock` and
  `packEndorserBlock`.
