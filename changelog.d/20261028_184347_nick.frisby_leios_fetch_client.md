### Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Jobs.jobCommitmentOfTxHashes`
  becomes `jobCommitmentOfTxs`: a job's commitment covers each transaction's
  size as well as its hash.

### Non-Breaking

- New pipelined LeiosFetch client (`leiosFetchClientPeerPipelined`), with
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Client.Replies`: it sends the
  requests assigned to its peer, checks and writes the replies, and emits
  each acquisition once its write is durable. A peer that replies as an honest
  one would not is disconnected by an `ExnLeiosFetchInvalidReply`, including
  one that serves a transaction of another size than its endorser block
  declares.
- The client writes a body together with the transactions it references that
  the node's mempool holds (`lfcMempoolTxs`), so it fetches none of them, and
  traces where each body's transactions come from
  (`TraceLeiosFetchClientBlockTxSources`).
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Trace` gains
  `TraceLeiosFetchClientEvent`, `FetchArrivalBytes` and
  `LeiosTxCacheInsertBodySummary`, which
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.TxCache.API` re-exports.
