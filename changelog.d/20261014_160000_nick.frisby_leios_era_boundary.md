### Breaking

- An endorser block may not be certified across an era boundary.
  `PolyPraosState`'s `praosStateLeiosAnnouncement` holds a
  `Strict.Either WhyNotCertifiable LeiosAnnouncement`: the announcement the next
  header may certify, or why it may certify none. `PolyPraosValidationErr`
  gains `LeiosCertAcrossEraBoundary`.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Protocol.Abstract` drops the degenerate
  `TranslateProto p p era` instance. `TPraos` and `Praos` each have an identity
  instance instead, and `Praos2` one that marks the pending announcement as
  made in the previous era.
