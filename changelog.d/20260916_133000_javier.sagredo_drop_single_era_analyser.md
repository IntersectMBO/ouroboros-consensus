### Breaking

- `Cardano.Tools.DBAnalyser.Block.Byron` and
  `Cardano.Tools.DBAnalyser.Block.Shelley` no longer export anything. Gone are
  their `HasProtocolInfo` instances, `ByronBlockArgs`, `ShelleyBlockArgs` and
  `openGenesisByron`, which only the Byron instance used. Both modules remain,
  imported for the per-era `HasAnalysis` instances that db-analyser dispatches
  to.

  These were the Byron-only and Shelley-only modes, neither of them reachable:
  the command line only ever builds a `CardanoBlockArgs`.
