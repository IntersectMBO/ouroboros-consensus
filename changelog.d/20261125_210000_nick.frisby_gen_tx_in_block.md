### Breaking

- Add `GenTxInBlock` for full transactions extracted from blocks. `HasTxs`
  now provides `extractTxs` and an explicit `txInBlockToMempoolTx` conversion.
  `extractAsMempoolTxs` remains available as a convenience function. Dijkstra
  conversion resets the phase-2 validity claim as fresh submission decoding
  does; extraction preserves the original transaction and its flag.
- Add `OneEraGenTxInBlock` for the HFC representation and support unary HFC
  injection and projection of full transactions.
