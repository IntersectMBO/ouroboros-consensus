### Breaking

- The VolatileDB's `BlockInfo` gains `biLeios`, what the block says about Leios
  endorser blocks (`LeiosBlockInfo`), and the `VolatileDB` gains
  `getLeiosAnnouncers`. `VolatileDbArgs` gains `volLeiosBlockInfo`, which
  `completeChainDbArgs` sets.
- `extractBlockInfo` takes a function giving a block's `LeiosBlockInfo`.
- `completeChainDbArgs` requires `BlockSupportsProtocol blk` and
  `LeiosProtocol (BlockProtocol blk)`.
- `TraceAddBlockEvent` gains `ReconsideredParkedBlocks`.
- `LeiosDbHandle`'s `leiosDbGarbageCollect` is now `garbageCollect`.

### Non-Breaking

- ChainSel parks a block containing a Leios certificate until the LeiosDB holds
  the closure of the endorser block it certifies, and reconsiders it once the
  LeiosDB reports that closure complete.
