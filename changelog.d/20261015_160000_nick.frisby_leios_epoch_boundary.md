### Breaking

- An endorser block may not be certified across an epoch boundary either.
  `WhyNotCertifiable` gains `AnnouncedInPreviousEpoch`, which
  `tickChainDepStatePolyPraos` sets when it ticks into a new epoch.
  `tickChainDepStatePolyPraos` now requires `Functor (WhenLeios proto)`.
- `PolyPraosValidationErr`'s `LeiosCertWithoutAnnouncement` carries the
  `WhyNotCertifiable` reason, the predecessor's slot and the header's slot. It
  replaces `LeiosCertAcrossEraBoundary`.
- `WhyNotCertifiable` is a `Semigroup` that keeps the more specific reason, and
  the new `markUncertifiable` uses it. Both the epoch tick and the `Praos2`
  era translation mark the pending announcement through it, so neither
  overwrites the other's more specific reason, whichever runs first.
