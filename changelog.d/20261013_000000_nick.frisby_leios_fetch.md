### Breaking

- `Codecs`, `Apps`, `ByteLimits` and `Handlers` in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode` gain fields for the LeiosFetch
  mini-protocol, which runs as a hot mini-protocol on `NodeToNodeV_16` and
  later.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode.LeiosNotify`'s `leiosNotifyProtocols`
  becomes `leiosNotifyMiniProtocol`, which does not check the version.
- `NodeKernelArgs` gains `leiosDB` and `NodeKernel` gains `getLeiosDB`.
  `mkNodeKernelArgs` takes the LeiosDB after the ChainDB, and
  `LowLevelRunNodeArgs` gains `llrnWithLeiosDB`.
- The ChainDB's `ChainDbSpecificArgs` gains `cdbsLeiosDb`, which has no
  default. `completeChainDbArgs` and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.openChainDB`
  take the LeiosDB after the LedgerDB's backend arguments.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Protocol.LeiosProtocol` gains
  `getContainsLeiosCert`, whether a header's block contains a Leios
  certificate. `defaultGetContainsLeiosCert` gives it for a protocol without
  Leios.
- The ChainDB's `openDB` and `withDB` require
  `LeiosProtocol (BlockProtocol blk)`. Before copying blocks to the
  ImmutableDB, the ChainDB pins in the LeiosDB the endorser blocks they
  certify. Its garbage collection drives the LeiosDB's.
- `Handlers`' `hLeiosFetchServer` takes the connection's LeiosDB reader.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.Tracers.Tracers` gains `leiosFetchServerTracer`,
  whose events (`TraceLeiosFetchServerEvent`, in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.Trace`) the `tracing` library
  renders.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode.Tracers` gains `tLeiosFetchTracer`,
  for the messages LeiosFetch sends and receives.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Network.NodeToNode.Tracers` gains `tLeiosNotifyTracer`,
  for the messages LeiosNotify sends and receives.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.Tracers.Tracers` gains `leiosDbTracer`, which the
  node's LeiosDB traces to. The `tracing` library renders its events
  (`TraceLeiosDb`).

### Non-Breaking

- `ModChainSel p t` is a `LeiosProtocol` when `p` is.
- New LeiosFetch mini-protocol, in
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.MiniProtocol.LeiosFetch.*`, numbered as the
  cardano-blueprint numbers it. The node's client requests nothing yet. Its
  server serves endorser blocks and their transactions from the LeiosDB, and
  disconnects a client that requests one it does not hold.
- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types` gains `LeiosTx`, `decodeLeiosEb`,
  `maxLeiosEbBytesSize` and `maxTxsPerEb`.
- The node opens a LeiosDB: `leios.vol.db` beside the VolatileDB, and
  `leios.imm.db` beside the ImmutableDB.
- The in-memory LeiosDB's `writeEbBody` throws for a point not yet written,
  as the SQLite one's does.
