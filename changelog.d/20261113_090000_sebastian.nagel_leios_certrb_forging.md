### Breaking

- `ForgeBlockArgs` gains `fbLeiosCert`, the certificate of the endorser block
  the block's predecessor announced, if the block certifies it.
- `forge` takes a function `RealPoint blk -> LeiosAnnouncement -> m (Maybe LeiosCert)`,
  before the endorser block callback. It gives the certificate for the
  announcement of the block at that point. `forge` calls it only when
  `leiosAnnouncementToCertify` gives its predecessor's announcement, and the
  block certifies when it gives a certificate.
  Such a block carries no transactions and announces no endorser block.
- A block certifies only once the node holds the certified closure.
- `ChainDB` gains `getIsLeiosClosureAcquired`.
