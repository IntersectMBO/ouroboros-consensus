### Breaking

- The LedgerDB applies a block containing a Leios certificate after the endorser block it certifies, when validating, replaying and in `reapplyThenPushNOW`.
  The endorser block applies on the state its predecessor left, without validating its transactions, and its differences come first.
  `LedgerDB.openDB`, `V2.mkInitDb`, `ValidateArgs` and `V2.reapplyThenPush` take a `ResolveEndorserBlock`, which the ChainDB reads from the LeiosDB.

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `ledgerAnnouncedLeiosPoint`, the endorser block the ledger records its tip announced, and `applyEndorserBlock`, which decodes and applies that endorser block, reading the values it needs through the given function.
  Block types without endorser blocks use `defaultLedgerAnnouncedLeiosPoint` and `defaultApplyEndorserBlock`.
  Dijkstra applies with the ledger's `applyEndorserBlockNoValidation`, and the hard fork combinator dispatches on the era of the state.
  The `HardForkBlock` instance moved to `Ouroboros.Consensus.HardFork.Combinator.Ledger` and needs `HasCanonicalTxIn` and `HasHardForkTxOut`.

- `LeiosEraDict` requires `DijkstraApplyBlock` and a decoder of the era's transactions.

- `ChainDbFailure` gains `ChainDbMissingEndorserBlock`.
  Like a missing block, it shuts the node down and triggers full validation on the next start.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `SerialisedEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`, `getKnownEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Query`, and `ResolveEndorserBlock`, `ExnUndecodableEndorserBlock` and `applyCertifiedEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LedgerDB.Forker`.
