### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `applyLeiosClosure`, which applies the closure of the endorser block a ledger state announced, without validating its transactions.
  Dijkstra implements it with the ledger's `applyEndorserBlockNoValidation`, and the hard fork combinator dispatches on the state's era.
  The `HardForkBlock` instance moved to `Ouroboros.Consensus.HardFork.Combinator.Ledger` and needs `HasCanonicalTxIn` and `HasHardForkTxOut`.

- `LeiosEraDict` requires the era to be Dijkstra.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `LeiosClosure`, `ResolveLeiosClosure` and `toLedgerEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`.
