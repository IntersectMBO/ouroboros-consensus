### Breaking

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `decodeLedgerEndorserBlock`, `getEndorserBlockKeySets`, `ledgerAnnouncedEb` and `applyLedgerEndorserBlock`, over a new data family `LedgerEndorserBlock`.
  They apply the endorser block a ledger state announced, without validating its transactions.
  Dijkstra implements them with the ledger's `applyEndorserBlockNoValidation`, and the hard fork combinator dispatches on the era.
  The `HardForkBlock` instance moved to `Ouroboros.Consensus.HardFork.Combinator.Ledger` and needs `HasCanonicalTxIn` and `HasHardForkTxOut`.

- `LeiosEraDict` requires `DijkstraApplyBlock`.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `ApplyEndorserBlock` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsLeios`, which lifts the endorser block methods to `ExtLedgerState`.

- Added `LeiosClosure` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`.
