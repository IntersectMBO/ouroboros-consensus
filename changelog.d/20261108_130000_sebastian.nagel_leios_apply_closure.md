### Breaking

- The LedgerDB applies a block containing a Leios certificate after the endorser block it certifies, when validating, replaying and in `reapplyThenPushNOW`.
  The endorser block applies on the state its predecessor left, without validating its transactions, and its differences come first.
  `LedgerDB.openDB`, `V2.mkInitDb`, `ValidateArgs` and `V2.reapplyThenPush` take a `ResolveEndorserBlock`, which the ChainDB reads from the LeiosDB.

- `BlockSupportsLeios` gains `ledgerAnnouncedEb`, `decodeLedgerEndorserBlock`, `getEndorserBlockKeySets` and `applyLedgerEndorserBlock`, over an associated `LedgerEndorserBlock`.
  `ledgerAnnouncedEb` is the endorser block the ledger records its tip announced, and block types without endorser blocks use `defaultLedgerAnnouncedEb`.
  Dijkstra implements them with the ledger's `applyEndorserBlockNoValidation`, and the hard fork combinator dispatches on the era.
  The `HardForkBlock` instance moved to `Ouroboros.Consensus.HardFork.Combinator.Ledger` and needs `HasCanonicalTxIn` and `HasHardForkTxOut`.

- `LeiosEraDict` requires `DijkstraApplyBlock` and a decoder of the era's transactions.

- `ChainDbFailure` gains `ChainDbMissingEndorserBlock` and `ChainDbUndecodableEndorserBlock`.
  Like a missing block, they shut the node down and trigger full validation on the next start.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `ApplyEndorserBlock` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsLeios`, which reads the endorser block the tip of a `LedgerState` or `ExtLedgerState` announced and applies it, and `certifiedEndorserBlock`, the endorser block a block certifies.

- Added `LeiosClosure` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Leios.Types`, `lookupLeiosClosure` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LeiosDB.API`, `getKnownEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.ChainDB.Impl.Query`, and `ResolveEndorserBlock` and `applyCertifiedEndorserBlock` to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Storage.LedgerDB.Forker`.
