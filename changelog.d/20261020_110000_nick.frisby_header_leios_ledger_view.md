### Breaking

- `HeaderWithTime` and `HeaderStateWithTime` carry the Leios part of the
  ledger view at their slot, and `HeaderWithTime` carries the slot of its
  predecessor.
- `mkHeaderWithTime` is removed. The headers of the ChainDB's selection come
  from validating their blocks: `SuccessForkerAction` receives them,
  `ValidateSuccessful` carries them, and `validateFork` requires
  `ExtLedgerState`.
- `mkHeaderStateWithTimeFromSummary` takes the ledger config and the extended
  ledger state.
- `Test.Util.Header.attachSlotTime` requires a block whose ledger view is
  trivial, and takes the slot of the header's predecessor.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsProtocol` gains `leiosLedgerViewAtTip`.
