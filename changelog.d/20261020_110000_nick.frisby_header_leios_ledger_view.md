### Breaking

- `HeaderStateWithTime` carries the Leios part of the ledger view at its
  slot. If a `HeaderWithTime` claims that its block contains a Leios
  certificate, it carries what checking that certificate takes
  (`hwtCertRbInfo`; see the new `certRbInfo`): the new `CertRbInfo` holds
  the slot of the header's predecessor, the announcement the certificate
  certifies, and the Leios part of the ledger view at the header's slot.
- `mkHeaderWithTime` is removed. The headers of the ChainDB's selection come
  from validating their blocks: `SuccessForkerAction` receives them,
  `ValidateSuccessful` carries them, and `validateFork` requires
  `ExtLedgerState`.
- `mkHeaderStateWithTimeFromSummary` takes the ledger config and the extended
  ledger state.

### Non-Breaking

- `Ouroboros.Consensus.Ledger.SupportsProtocol` gains `leiosLedgerViewAtTip`.
